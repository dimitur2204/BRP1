This is a project for a research bachelor's degree on extracting medically useful data from currently existing CT scans.

Inspired and based on an trying to work off of two white paper studies:
* https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10242-y#code-availability
* https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10243-x#code-availability

Different tests and ideas are introduced and each one is commited separately on a branch.

**Always read `PLAN.md` (staged plan + status) and `PROGRESS.md` (detailed
running log of what was built/found/decided) before starting work.** Update
both after any real progress — don't duplicate their content here.
