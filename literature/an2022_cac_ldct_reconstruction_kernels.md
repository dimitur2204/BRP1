# Evaluating coronary artery calcification with low-dose chest CT reconstructed by different kernels

- **Authors:** An S, Fan R, Zhao B, Yi Q, Yao S, Shi X, Zhu Y, Yi X, Liu S
- **Year / venue:** 2022, *Clinical Imaging* 83:166–171
- **DOI:** 10.1016/j.clinimag.2021.12.024 · PMID 35074625
- **How verified:** PubMed record and abstract.

## Key findings
- Participants in the NELCIN-B3 screening programme had both a dedicated calcium-scoring CT and an LDCT. The LDCT was reconstructed with smooth, standard and sharp kernels.
- LDCT detected CAC with 98.5% sensitivity (330/335) regardless of kernel.
- **The smooth kernel agreed best** with dedicated calcium CT: ICC for volume 0.932, mass 0.904, Agatston 0.906, and risk-category κ 0.757. It also had the lowest noise and best SNR/CNR.
- LDCT effective dose was 41.2% lower than the calcium scan.

## Relevance to our project
- This supports quantifying calcium mass on soft-kernel LDCT and explains why our fixed 130 HU threshold failed on sharp kernels. Kernel group (or measured noise) should stay a covariate or stratification factor in every calcium analysis, and soft-kernel series should be preferred where available.
