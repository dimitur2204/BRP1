# Deep Learning to Estimate Biological Age From Chest Radiographs

- **Authors:** Raghu VK, Weiss J, Hoffmann U, Aerts HJWL, Lu MT
- **Year / venue:** 2021, *JACC: Cardiovascular Imaging* 14(11):2226–2236
- **DOI:** 10.1016/j.jcmg.2021.01.008 · PMID 33744131 · PMCID PMC11381093
- **How verified:** PubMed record and abstract.

## Key findings
- CXR-Age, an imaging "ageing clock", is a CNN estimating biological age from a chest radiograph. It was developed on 116,035 people and tested in PLCO (n = 40,967) and **NLST (n = 5,414)**.
- PLCO: a 5-year increase in CXR-Age gave all-cause mortality HR 2.26 (2.24–2.29), versus HR 1.77 for 5 years of chronological age. For CV mortality: 2.45 vs 1.82.
- Similar results in NLST. Adding CXR-Age to a model with chronological age, risk factors and radiograph findings improved prediction of both outcomes (P < 0.001).

## Relevance to our project
- Our "immune age" (thymus) and "vascular age" (calcium) are organ-specific versions of this whole-image ageing clock. If both markers carry independent signal, a composite "CT biological age" gap is a natural secondary analysis.
- It also warns that any image-derived ageing marker partly re-encodes chronological age and sex, so our thymus–calcium correlations must be age/sex-partialled.
