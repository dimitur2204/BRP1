# AI for Multistructure Incidental Findings and Mortality Prediction at Chest CT in Lung Cancer Screening

- **Authors:** Marcinkiewicz AM, Buchwald M, Shanbhag A, Bednarski BP, Killekar A, Miller RJH, Builoff V, Lemley M, Berman DS, Dey D, Slomka PJ
- **Year / venue:** 2024, *Radiology* 312(3):e240541
- **DOI:** 10.1148/radiol.240541 · PMID 39287522 · PMCID PMC11427857
- **How verified:** PubMed record and abstract.

## Key findings
- NLST baseline LDCT, n = 24,401. **32 structures segmented** per scan, 15 interpretable radiomic features per structure, plus AI CAC and epicardial fat, fed into ensemble ML.
- 10-year all-cause mortality AUC 0.72, 2-year 0.71. Detection of extrapulmonary significant incidental findings AUC 0.70.
- **CAC had the highest feature importance** for all three endpoints.
- Lung-cancer incidence was not an endpoint.

## Relevance to our project
- This is the closest methodological analogue to our multi-organ radiomics on NLST. It confirms that cardiac calcium dominates extrapulmonary CT signal, consistent with our finding that only calcium replicated among about 20 heart features.
- It leaves a gap: multi-organ opportunistic markers (heart, thymus, fat) have not been evaluated for **lung-cancer incidence** in NLST.
