# Measuring Computed Tomography Scanner Variability of Radiomics Features

- **Authors:** Mackin D, Fave X, Zhang L, Fried D, Yang J, Taylor B, Rodriguez-Rivera E, Dodge C, Jones AK, Court L
- **Year / venue:** 2015, *Investigative Radiology* 50(11):757–765
- **DOI:** 10.1097/RLI.0000000000000180 · PMID 26115366 · PMCID PMC4598251
- **How verified:** PubMed record and abstract.

## Key findings
- A radiomics phantom with 10 cartridges was scanned on 17 CT scanners (GE, Philips, Siemens, Toshiba) at 4 centres using their routine thoracic protocols, and compared with features from 20 NSCLC tumours.
- Interscanner variability was **comparable to the between-patient variability** of NSCLC tumours for some features.
- Among NGTDM texture features, "feature noise" was greatest for busyness and least for texture strength. Phantom scans clustered partly by manufacturer.
- Conclusion: interscanner differences must be minimised or accounted for in radiomics studies.

## Relevance to our project
- This matches our R3 finding that texture features were kernel- and manufacturer-dominated and that no texture hit replicated. It is a citation for why we trust only simple, physically defined features (calcium mass/volume, fat volume) over texture.
